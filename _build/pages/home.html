FAQ

Common questions before you call.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We provide free on-site estimates, and every quote is itemized so you can see exactly what's included.

Which towns do you serve?

We're based in Sandwich, MA and work across Cape Cod, from the Upper Cape all the way to Provincetown, plus Plymouth. See our service areas, or tell us your town or ZIP and we'll confirm.

Do you repair, or only replace?

Both. If a repair will hold up, we'll recommend it. For decks, for example, boards can sometimes be replaced without a rebuild when the framing is solid. We'll check and tell you honestly.

What window and door types do you install?

Double-hung, casement, sliders, picture windows, entry doors, and sliding patio doors. We can also replace a single unit and match the existing style and size.

Wood or composite for my deck?

We build with both. During the estimate we'll walk you through the trade-offs in look, maintenance, and budget for your specific spot.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope. After the site visit you'll get a realistic timeline in writing, and we keep to it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're licensed (HIC/CSL) and insured in Massachusetts, and BBB accredited. A certificate of insurance is available on request.
